📋 SETUP (One-time, 5 minutes):

1. Setup cron job (automated hourly price checks):
   ./setup_cron.sh

2. Start trading bot (collects data):
   nohup python3 trader.py > trading.out 2>&1 &

3. Verify running:
   ps aux | grep trader.py
